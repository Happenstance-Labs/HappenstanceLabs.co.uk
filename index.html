UK politics, made understandable

Westminster should be easy to follow.

Parliament decides things that shape everyday life, but it rarely explains itself well. We make apps that help people understand how it works, keep up with what happens there, and have some fun along the way.

Plain English

We explain the jargon instead of hiding behind it, so you don't need to know how Parliament works before you start.

Non-partisan

We don't back parties, policies or MPs. We show what happened and leave the opinions to you.

Built on the record

Our apps use official Parliament data, and we say where every figure comes from.

ChamberWise

Learn how Parliament works

A friendly guide to Westminster. Short lessons and daily games teach you how laws get made, and you can look up your MP and your area to see what's happening there.

  • Daily brief: what happened in Parliament yesterday, in a few minutes.
  • Lessons and games: bite-size learning, with streaks and XP to keep you coming back.
  • Your MP and your area: votes, bills, petitions and local statistics in one place.
  • Leagues: compete with friends, classmates or colleagues.

Fantasy Westminster

New · 2026–27 season

Fantasy football, but the players are MPs. Pick a squad, set your line-up before each week's deadline, and score points for the work your MPs do in the House of Commons.

  • Pick 11 MPs on a £100m budget, with no more than three from any one party.
  • Points for the work: debates, questions, committee sessions and turning up to vote.
  • Never for how they vote. An Aye scores the same as a No, so no side is favoured.
  • Leagues and transfers: captain your star MP, make transfers, and beat your mates.

About us

Happenstance Labs is a small, independent studio in the UK. We think more people would take an interest in Parliament if it were easier to get into, so that's what we work on.

Everything we make uses official UK Parliament data, published under the Open Parliament Licence. We're not affiliated with Parliament, any party or any campaign.
